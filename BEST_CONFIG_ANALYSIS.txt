✅ RECOMMENDED CONFIGURATION:

Optimizer:  SGD Nesterov
  - lr: 0.01 (higher than Adam's 0.001)
  - momentum: 0.9
  - nesterov: true
  - weight_decay: 0.0001

Scheduler: ReduceLROnPlateau (RLRP)
  - factor: 0.5 (reduce LR by 50% when plateau)
  - patience: 5 (wait 5 epochs)
  - min_lr: 0.00001 (minimum threshold)

Loss function: cross_entropy with label_smoothing=0.1

Expected Results:
  ✓ Validation: ~73.6%
  ✓ Testing: ~73.0%
  ✓ Minimal overfitting (only -0.6% gap)


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY THIS COMBINATION IS BEST:

1. SGD Nesterov advantages:
   - Simpler, more stable convergence
   - Better generalization (less overfitting)
   - Works well with images/CNNs
   - Nesterov gives lookahead gradient (better convergence)

2. ReduceLROnPlateau advantages:
   - Adaptive: only reduces LR when needed
   - Smallest gap between val & test (0.6%)
   - No need to predict epoch schedule
   - Highest test accuracy (73.0%)

3. Why NOT other combinations:
   - Adam: Best for quick convergence, but worse gen (72.2% test)
   - Constant LR: High overfitting (test drops to 71.3%)
   - StepLR: Fixed schedule may be suboptimal
   - Cosine: Smooth decay but not adaptive
